## Sports Analytics Literature & Research

- [Pitch sequence effectiveness via visual deception metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35714) (22/30) — Statcast tunneling; DAS/AAS angular-separation features lift out-of-sample R² for FB→BR/OS sequences.

- [Grad-CAM shot classification shortcut reliance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33263) (15/30) and [Unified trajectory + 3D pose forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30519) (15/30) — CV/XAI, low practicality for a play-by-play stack.
