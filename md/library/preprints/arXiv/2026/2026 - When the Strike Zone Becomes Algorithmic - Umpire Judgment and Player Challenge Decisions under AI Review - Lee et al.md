# When the Strike Zone Becomes Algorithmic: Umpire Judgment and Player Challenge Decisions under AI Review

**Kichang Lee, Gyeongmin Han, Sungmin Lee, JeongGil Ko**

arXiv:[2609.25525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25525) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25525)

## Triage (N/P/R = 6/6/8, composite 20/30)

MLB 2026 ABS challenge system: 4.11M called pitches 2015-26 + 8,447 challenges. Effective called boundary moved toward the automated zone; umpires adjust briefly after overturns; players leave many overturnable calls unchallenged. Statcast data (public). PDF not mirrored (arXiv license).
