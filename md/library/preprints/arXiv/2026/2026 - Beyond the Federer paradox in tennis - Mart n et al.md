# Beyond the Federer paradox in tennis

- Authors: Andrés Martín, Johann H. Martínez, Javier M. Buldú
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03702
- Sport: Tennis
- Score: N7/P6/R8 = 21/30 (abstract-level triage)

## Takeaway

Winning with fewer points (Federer: 80% of matches on 54% of points) follows from hierarchical scoring plus crucial-point leverage; amplification is set by points per match; 4.7% of ATP 1991-2025 matches were won with fewer points, more than an iid-points model predicts.
