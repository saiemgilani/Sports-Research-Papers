# Identifying the determinants of soccer coach turnover by competing risks models: a case study on Italian League Serie A

- Authors: Francesco Porro, Marialuisa Restaino, Juan Eloy Ruiz-Castro, Mariangela Zenga
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04572
- Sport: Soccer
- Score: N6/P6/R5 = 17/30 (abstract-level triage)

## Takeaway

Competing-risks (cause-specific Cox, LASSO, Random Survival Forests) model of Serie A head-coach exits 2010-2019; recent performance (points/match) drives sacking, personal traits matter little once performance is controlled.
