# Throwing4: Phase-Aware Pose Modeling for Throw-Distance Prediction in Elite Athletics

**Bikash Kumar Badatya, Kartike Tiwari, Jyotirmoy Amin, Ravi Hegde**

arXiv:[2610.16383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16383) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16383)

## Triage (N/P/R = 6/4/8, composite 18/30)

500 broadcast throwing clips (javelin, discus, hammer, shot put) with 2D pose, phase labels and official distances; phase-conditioned encoders reduce distance-prediction error across graph pose backbones. Dataset on GitHub. PDF not mirrored (arXiv license).
