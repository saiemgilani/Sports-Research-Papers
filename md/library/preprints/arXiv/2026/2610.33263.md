                                                                    The applied study in this paper provides important new in-
                                                                    sights. Our work bridges computer vision, XAI and sports
                                                                    analytics. We offer: i) explanation workflows adaptable to
                                                                    other CNN-based action recognition models; ii) possible ad-
                                                                    vances to sports analytics by auditing shortcut learning in
                                                                    models to reduce unfair penalization; iii) a role for XAI in
                                                                    any spatiotemporal setting to identify where learning meth-
                                                                    ods have weaknesses, towards improved designs.
                                                                       Future work will compare shortcut learning across archi-
                                                                    tectures. For example, vision transformers may rely on spuri-
                                                                    ous correlations such as the crowd to a lesser extent. Further,
                                                                    deep learning models can be trained and tested with shortcut
                                                                    mitigation efforts such as masking out the scoreboard, crowd,
                                                                    and other potential spurious correlations.
