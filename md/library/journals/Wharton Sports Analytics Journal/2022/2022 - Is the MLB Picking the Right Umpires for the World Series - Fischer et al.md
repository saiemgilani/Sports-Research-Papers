Is the MLB Picking the Right Umpires for the World Series?

By: Brooks Fischer, Ethan Schulman, Hayden Lee, Ethan Jin

Wharton Moneyball Academy Training Camp 2022, Sports Analytics Student Research Journal

Hypothesis: The MLB is not picking the right umpires for the World Series: The umpires that they are picking are too old.

## Conclusion

While older umpires might still be fairly consistent, overall older umpires are less accurate and should not be chosen for the World Series just because of their “experience”
