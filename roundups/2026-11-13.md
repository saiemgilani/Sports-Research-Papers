**Takeaways**
- The Glicko paper is the week's standout. It addresses the cross-league comparison problem that Elo/SPI-style club ratings handle poorly, and it is fully reproducible. Two direct uses: as a baseline rating for international club competitions, and as a template for league-aware initialization in college sports, where conference silos create the same problem.
- Rating models are a theme this month. With 2611.08856 and 2611.07335 (Bayesian competitive-balance tiers) and 2609.21674 (Elo vs Transfermarkt), the library now holds four 2026 papers that treat team strength as a latent rating with an explicit uncertainty model.
