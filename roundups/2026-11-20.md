## 1. Sports Analytics Literature & Research

**New this run: 2 papers**, both from Friday's `/new` listing (20 Nov). Both are simulation or tracking methods, not game-outcome work.

- [MyoChallenge 2025: A New Benchmark for Human Athletic Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15650) (Wang et al., 24 authors; **19/30**, N7/P3/R9).
  - **Setup:** this is the NeurIPS 2025 challenge built on MyoSuite physiological models. It had two tracks: a table-tennis rally (arm, hand and trunk) and a soccer penalty kick against a goalkeeper (legs and trunk, 290 muscle excitations, 418-dimensional observation).
  - **Results:** about 70 teams made more than 560 submissions. Off-the-shelf PPO and SAC succeed 0–2% of the time. The table-tennis winner (ActingAI) reached 100% in phase 1 and 64% in phase 2, and the top three finished within 5 points of each other. In soccer, only one team beat the baselines (Servette MyoClub). It fine-tuned KINESIS, a gait policy trained by motion imitation, rather than training from scratch.
  - **Why it matters here:** this is an open, reproducible testbed for muscle-level sports biomechanics. The environments are on GitHub (`MyoHub/myochallenge_2025eval`), and several winning teams released their code. The direct value to an analyst is low, so P=3.
- [SpecFlow: Flow Matching in the Acceleration Spectrum for Multi-Agent Trajectory Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26390) (Xu, Han & Zhou; **16/30**, N6/P4/R6).
  - **Method:** conditional flow matching generates the DCT coefficients of future *accelerations*. Velocity and position follow by fixed integration from the last observed state. An endpoint predictor proposes destinations from observed context only.
  - **Results:** on NBA SportVU (all 11 entities forecast jointly, best-of-20), it reaches 0.69/0.86 m minADE/minFDE at 4 s. That is the lowest 4-second ADE and a tied-best FDE, though IAD leads at 2 s. It also sets state of the art on ETH/UCY. Per-scene latency on NBA falls from 17.55 to 3.68 ms.
  - **Caveat:** best-of-20 scores reward covering possible futures, not making one usable forecast. Code is promised.

## 2. Open-Source Packages & Ecosystem Updates

- **sportsdataverse-py:** no release. Stable is [0.1.4](https://pypi.org/project/sportsdataverse/), with the pre-release 0.2.0b1. Six merges on 11-19 (#1244–#1249), mostly box-score conventions:
  - **CFB:** sacks and kneel-downs count as rushes in the box score, the NCAA convention (#1245).
  - **NFL:** kneels count as rushes; interception returns are left out of passing yards; net pass yards are added (#1247). The intended receiver is credited on an interception, and a text fragment is never taken as a receiver (#1248).
