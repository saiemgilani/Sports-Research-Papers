## 1. Sports Analytics Literature & Research

**New this run: 1 paper.**
- [Match classification in the last round of four-team round-robin tournaments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25610) (Csató & Gyimesi; **18/30**, N6/P6/R6). First submitted in May; the v3 revision was announced on Sunday evening (2026-11-15 ET) in physics.soc-ph, stat.AP and econ.GN. The library had no copy, so it is saved now. The paper compares a deterministic classifier of last-round group matches (4 types) with a probabilistic one (6 types, weighing the gains from attacking against defending). All six types occurred at the 2018 World Cup. It then splits the 2026 reform into three parts. Going to 48 teams lowers competitive balance. Letting the best third-placed teams qualify turns "antagonistic" matches, where a draw suits one side, into "offensive" ones, where a draw suits nobody. Head-to-head tie-breakers reduce competitiveness. The result applies to any group of four with two qualifiers, which covers the FIBA World Cup, the Euros, and the handball and volleyball world championships.
