## 1. Sports Analytics Literature & Research

**Three new papers today** (11-25 submissions). Two are NBA papers on public data, the strongest day for reproducible basketball work this month.

- **[Who Fills the Gap? Role Similarity and Teammates' Minutes When NBA Players Are Ruled Out](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34688)** (Guan & Zhou, stat.AP). **21/30** (N6 P7 R8).
  - **Data:** 2,441 team-games, 2023-24 to 2025-26, where a core player was ruled out for a medical reason on the last official injury report stamped at least 6 h before tip (23,485 report PDFs parsed). Box scores, pbp-derived lineup stints and closing spreads come from ESPN game summaries.
  - **Result:** among teammates in the same game, one within-case SD more similarity to the absent set (3PA, assists and rebounds per minute) goes with about **0.63 more minutes** (p < 0.0001), and it replicates in two earlier seasons.
  - **The useful negative:** that association gives **no detectable reduction in minute-forecast error**. "Role coverage" (how much of the missing role the rotation can restore) has no consistent link to team output or final margin. Whether similarity adds anything beyond listed position is unresolved.
  - **Availability:** code, unit tests, analysis plans with commit history, a SHA-256 manifest of every source file and four derived tables. The 2026-27 replication is pre-registered ([OSF](https://osf.io/kzfyu), embargoed).
- **[Shooting for Fit: Risk-Averse Stochastic Roster Construction from Bayesian Shot-Pattern Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33820)** (Yu, Zhang, Crandall & Hu, stat.AP). **20/30** (N7 P7 R6).
  - **Data:** nba_api pbp, shot locations and rotations for 2015-16 to 2025-26 (1.88M shots in 2015-24); salaries and moves from Basketball-Reference; the 2011/2017/2023 CBA rules encoded.
  - **Method:** shots fall into 25 cells (5 zones x drive/cut/pull-up/post/spot-up). A Bayesian model finds seven roles and how teammates shift each player's shot mix and efficiency, giving uncertainty-quantified values for lineups that never played. A risk-averse two-stage stochastic MIP then picks acquisitions under the cap, with lineup minutes as the recourse.
  - **Result:** on 240 team-offseasons (2016-2023) plus two frozen held-out seasons, compatibility-aware plans differ from rating-based ones and would lift a league-average offense to about **sixth**. Flexible minutes absorb most of the role uncertainty.
  - **Limits:** offense only; no code link.
- **[Sports Agent: A Self-Evolving System for Conversational Data Analytics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33700)** (Chen et al., Alibaba). **11/30** (N5 P4 R2).
  - An agentic Qwen3.6-35B-A3B text-to-SQL model, a planning harness and a feedback engine that verifies claims, diagnoses failures and synthesises new training questions. It beats Qwen3.8-Max on sports QA at about 3x the inference efficiency; in production +10.5 points factual correctness, -41.3% latency.
  - Proprietary database, benchmark and weights, so low reproducibility. The bounded-update-with-regression-replay idea is the transferable part.
