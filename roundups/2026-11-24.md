## 1. Sports Analytics Literature & Research

**Two new papers today.** One is from Monday's 11-23 arXiv mailing. The other was announced 11-20 and missed by the daily sweeps.

- **[AI-Assisted Decision Support for Formula 1 Pit-Stop Strategy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26909)** (Méndez & Thraves, 11-19). **21/30** (N6 P7 R8). This is the week's top-scoring paper.
  - **Simulator:** calibrated on FastF1 + OpenF1 data for 2021–2024. It models tire degradation by compound, recent driver form, overtaking as a function of the gap, yellow-flag rates by circuit, and rivals' pit decisions.
  - **Comparison:** five DRL agents against a quadratic MIP. The QMIP is exact for a deterministic single-driver race.
  - **Results:** without uncertainty, every agent except TRPO is near-optimal. With a full stochastic grid, only PPO and Recurrent PPO beat the QMIP on average over eight 2024 circuits: PPO gains +0.33 positions in simulation and +0.49 when the real races are replayed. On replay, PPO finishes 0.66 places ahead of the strategies the drivers actually used.
  - **Look-ahead tool:** it shows the distribution of finishing positions and points for each pit choice, for example the safety-car lap at the 2024 Saudi GP.
  - **Limits:** rivals follow behavioural models and do not respond strategically. The tire sets available at each stop are inferred.
  - **Availability:** data and code are on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24361704).
- **[X-AQA: Unifying Action Quality Assessment with MLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28548)** (Zheng et al., 11-21). **17/30** (N6 P4 R7).
  - **Data:** 40,163 samples from 22 action-quality datasets, recast as question answering, with official scoring rules (FINA diving, ISU skating) put in the prompt. Competitive sport is 35% of the corpus: FineFS, MTL-AQA, AQA-7, rhythmic gymnastics, CricketVision and MultiSenseBadminton.
  - **Model:** a LoRA-tuned Qwen3.5-9B. In-distribution it beats every open MLLM and GPT-6.1 Sol (SRCC 0.607 vs 0.465).
  - **Against specialists:** on MTL-AQA diving it reaches 0.886 SRCC, while dedicated scorers reach about 0.96.
  - **Held-out data:** multi-domain training lifts SRCC on held-out datasets from 0.107 to 0.244, which is still weak.
  - **Verdict:** a generalist baseline, not a judging tool. Data and model are on Hugging Face.
