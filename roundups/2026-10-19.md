### Analysis and take-aways
- **Measurement-window leakage** (Hu & Wang) and **decision-readiness gates** (Zhou & Guan) both target the same failure: metrics that look explanatory because they share information with the outcome, or that predict but don't inform decisions. Suggest adding a *point-in-time validity / leakage check* to the rubric in `paper-ranking-report.md` (currently N/P/R only).
- **Credit-assignment is the dominant theme** (blocking HMM, Hawkes xGI, MPE action values): all replace all-or-nothing attribution with probabilistic partial credit. Only the Hawkes paper is fully reproducible from open data.
- **Public-data share this week:** 6 of 10 filed papers use public data; 1 ships code.
