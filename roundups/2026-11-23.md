## 1. Sports Analytics Literature & Research

**One new paper today**, from the Sunday 11-22 arXiv mailing (submissions of 11-20).

- **[The Science and Mythology of Sports Rankings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27886)** (Feldman, stat.AP). **14/30** (N4 P6 R4). The author ran Laxpower, a lacrosse ratings site, from 1997 to 2019, and wrote the paper with the NCAA Men's D-I Lacrosse Selection Criteria and Ranking Committee. It is a conceptual paper.
  - It separates four ranking tasks: prediction, retrospective evaluation, selection and seeding. A method that is good at one need not be good at another.
  - It gives 25 attributes in four clusters (trust, mathematical soundness, practicality, fairness) for judging RPI/NPI/NET/BCS-style systems against the purpose they serve.
  - Table 2 compares nine D-I sports. Schedules run from 12–13 games (FBS) and 15–16 (men's lacrosse) to 55–60 (baseball), and cross-conference play from 25% to 70%. The connectivity columns are qualitative estimates, not computed.
  - There is no model, no data release and no code. It is useful as a checklist for SDV ranking pages and selection-related features; it is not a method to adopt.
